<h2>Limits Workshops Must Respect</h2>

<p>ALLDATA AI does not:</p>
<ul>
<li>Replace oscilloscope or scan tool diagnosis</li>
<li>Guarantee coverage on grey imports or EU-only variants</li>
<li>Provide Identifix-style confirmed fixes from independent shops</li>
<li>Make monochrome wiring as readable as HaynesPro colour diagrams</li>
</ul>
