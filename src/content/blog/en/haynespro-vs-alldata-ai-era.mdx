<h2>Which AI Adapts Faster by Vehicle Type?</h2>

<p><strong>European volume (VW, BMW, PSA, Renault):</strong> HaynesPro's AI search returns relevant colour diagrams faster because the underlying dataset matches EU spec variants. ALLDATA can lag on engines or trim never sold in North America.</p>

<p><strong>US/Japanese (Ford, GM, Toyota, Honda):</strong> ALLDATA's AI indexing sits on OEM-licensed US data — TSB links and factory schematics align with what US insurers and dealers expect. HaynesPro is usable but thinner on some domestic platforms.</p>

<p><strong>Mixed independent garage:</strong> Neither AI alone is sufficient. Technicians switch sources by ticket — the win is one login that includes both.</p>
