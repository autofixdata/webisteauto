<h2>How to Use HaynesPro AI Wiring Search</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Start with the VIN or exact model variant</strong> — AI cannot fix wrong vehicle selection</li>
<li><strong>Search in plain language</strong> — e.g. "rear wiper circuit fuse" or "ECU ground G101"</li>
<li><strong>Open the suggested schematic</strong> — verify year and engine code match</li>
<li><strong>Follow linked connectors</strong> — use pin-out views before probing live circuits</li>
<li><strong>Cross-check power and ground paths</strong> — AI suggestions are starting points, not proof</li>
</ol>

<h2>Common Workshop Scenarios</h2>

<h3>Dead accessory circuit</h3>
<p>Search the affected system + "fuse" or "relay". HaynesPro typically surfaces the fuse box layout and circuit schematic together. Test fuse load side before replacing components upstream.</p>

<h3>U0100 / CAN communication codes</h3>
<p>Search the DTC or "CAN bus [module name]". AI may link related modules on the same bus. Use a scan tool to confirm which module is offline before chasing wiring.</p>

<h3>EV high-voltage interlock</h3>
<p>Always open the HV safety section first. Search "HV interlock" or "isolation". Never rely on AI summaries alone for orange-cable work — follow full OEM steps.</p>
