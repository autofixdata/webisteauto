<h2>Do You Need Manage AI?</h2>

<p><strong>Choose Manage AI if:</strong> you want one vendor for DMS + repair data and already use ALLDATA Manage daily.</p>

<p><strong>Skip Manage if:</strong> you have a separate workshop management system (Garage Hive, Tekmetric, etc.) and only need OEM data in the bay. A repair-data bundle like AutoFixData avoids duplicate DMS spend.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is ALLDATA Manage AI?</h3>
<p>AI-assisted features inside ALLDATA's shop management product — estimates, scheduling, communications — separate from Repair's technician-facing AI search.</p>

<h3>Can I use ALLDATA Repair AI without Manage?</h3>
<p>Yes. Repair subscriptions work independently. Manage is an optional upsell for full shop operations.</p>

<h3>Does AutoFixData include ALLDATA Manage?</h3>
<p>No — AutoFixData focuses on repair data access (Repair-level content plus HaynesPro, AutoData, Mitchell1, Identifix). Use your existing DMS for scheduling.</p>

<h3>Will Manage AI replace my service advisor?</h3>
<p>No. It drafts and suggests — advisors must approve estimates and customer-facing messages.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>

<p>ALLDATA Manage AI is for owners modernising a Manage-based shop. Technicians still need Repair AI (or equivalent) in the bay — and European wiring from HaynesPro. Split the decision: operations software vs repair data, then minimise overlap.</p>
