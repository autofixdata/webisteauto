<h2>TSB Workflow With ALLDATA AI</h2>

<ol>
<li>Record DTCs, freeze frame, and customer concern</li>
<li>Search ALLDATA AI with symptom + code (e.g. "P0301 TSB Ford F-150")</li>
<li>Open matching bulletins — check applicability (VIN range, build date)</li>
<li>Perform OEM test steps — do not skip pin tests because AI suggested a part</li>
<li>Document bulletin number on invoice for warranty defence</li>
</ol>
