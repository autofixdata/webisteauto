<h2>How AutoData Labour Times Work</h2>

<p>AutoData publishes OEM-derived operation times — engine, transmission, brakes, HVAC, and more. Times assume standard conditions; rust, seized fasteners, and missing trim add real-world hours.</p>

<h2>What AutoData AI Adds in 2026</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Fuzzy search</strong> — "timing belt Golf" without exact menu path</li>
<li><strong>Related operations</strong> — water pump with belt, bleed with brake pads</li>
<li><strong>Variant awareness prompts</strong> — engine code confirmation</li>
<li><strong>Service package hints</strong> — major vs minor service bundles</li>
</ul>

<h2>Common Quoting Mistakes</h2>

<ul>
<li>Using Car tier times for complex 4WD or ADAS-equipped vehicles</li>
<li>Ignoring sub-operations (battery disconnect, wheel bearing preload)</li>
<li>Matching wrong engine code — AI cannot fix wrong YMM</li>
<li>Forgetting MOT-related adjustments on failure rectification</li>
</ul>
